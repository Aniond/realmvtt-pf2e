Select your Character's Class.

All available Classes in the current Campaign will be shown in the dropdown below.

After completing this step, make any class choices from the prompts that appear (such as skills or class features). Your Class sets your starting level to 1, adds its Hit Points to your Ancestry's, and sets your Perception, save, skill, weapon, and armor proficiencies. Your Key Attribute boost is chosen in the Attribute step.

Your GM is using Level 0 Characters (GM Core). You start at level 0 with only your Ancestry and Background, and choose a Class when you reach 1st level.

Until then you are trained in Perception, all saving throws, unarmed attacks, unarmored defense, one simple weapon, and 2 + Intelligence modifier skills. Your Hit Points are your Ancestry's Hit Points plus your Constitution modifier, and you start with 5 gp.
