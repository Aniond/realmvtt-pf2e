If your class casts spells, choose your cantrips and spells. A spontaneous caster adds them to their repertoire. A wizard first fills their spellbook (a witch, their familiar), then prepares today's spells from it; a cleric or druid prepares from their whole spell list. Prepared spells can change at the next daily preparations.

You can skip any of them: on the Actions tab, the wand on an empty spell row picks a spell, or drag one in from the Spells compendium.
