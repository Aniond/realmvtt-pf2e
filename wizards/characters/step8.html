Your Ancestry, Background, Class, attributes, skills, languages, feats, and equipment are set. Finish your character from the sheet:

  • Equip: On the Inventory tab, equip your armor and shield and set which weapons you are holding so your AC and Strikes are correct.
  • Spells: If your Class casts spells, drag your cantrips and spells from the Spells Compendium to your Actions tab.
  • Details: From the Notes tab, set your Deity, age, appearance, and other personal details.
