Your Ancestry, Background, Class, attributes, skills, languages, and feats are set. Finish your character from the sheet:

  • Spells: If your Class casts spells, drag your cantrips and spells from the Spells Compendium to your Actions tab.
  • Equipment: A 1st-level character starts with 15 gp. Drag weapons, armor, and gear from the Items Compendium to your Inventory tab and spend it from there.
  • Details: From the Notes tab, set your Deity, age, appearance, and other personal details.
